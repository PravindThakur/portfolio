My Approach

Research first. Risk awareness always. Discipline throughout.

I follow equity and commodity markets, study price movements and turn research into trading decisions. I focus on understanding the market first, managing risk carefully and staying disciplined.

My day-to-day work in capital markets and corporate actions gives me a close view of how events such as dividends, stock splits and bonus issues are processed and how they can affect securities.

Market Research Study trends, price behaviour, market structure and relevant context.
Risk Management Consider exposure, position sizing and potential downside before a trade.
Discipline Use a consistent process, review decisions and learn from outcomes.
